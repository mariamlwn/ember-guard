# Ember Guard

**NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · Challenge: "Flame in Freefall: AI-Powered Fire Safety Insights from Microgravity Combustion Data" · Salamanca, Spain**

Ember Guard is a (digital) inspection robot concept for spacecraft. It flies to a cabin, grips a handrail, switches off its own thrusters so it does not disturb the airflow, measures the air, and **estimates the risk that a flame could be sustained** there, before there is any flame. It warns the crew with eye colour, voice and sound, proposes preventive actions (cut ventilation or power to a module) and acts automatically if the crew is asleep and does not respond. It never claims to detect fires.

**Live demo:** open `index.html` (single file, works offline). In Microsoft Edge it uses natural voices.

## What makes it different
- **Real NASA microgravity fire data.** The model is trained on all 122 usable tests of the ISS experiment BASS-II (Burning and Suppression of Solids II), transcribed line by line from the report's test matrices, with source and page on every row.
- **Real ISS cabin air, live.** "Use real ISS air" reads the US Lab's oxygen, CO₂, pressure and temperature from NASA's public ISS Live telemetry and runs the BASS-II model on the air astronauts are breathing now.
- **Explainable and honest.** A small logistic regression with 6 inputs; every estimate shows a 90 % uncertainty band, a "why this number" breakdown and an out-of-range warning.
- **Play a night shift.** One button replays a full scripted night: oxygen rises, ventilation slows, Ember cuts the module's ventilation on its own and sends a holographic report. Night and day modes.
- **Talk to Ember.** Closed-domain voice/text assistant that runs on board without internet: it answers only about air, flame risk and safety actions, runs "what if" questions through the model, and cites the source of every fact.

## Validation
GroupKFold (5 folds × 20 repeats) **by test**: every test is predicted by a model that never saw any of its rows. Held-out AUC 0.79, Brier score 0.150 vs 0.191 for a no-skill guess.

## Limits
Valid only for O₂ ≈ 13.5–22 %, airflow 0.4–47 cm/s, ~1 atm, and PMMA plastics and SIBAL cotton-fiberglass fabric. CO₂, temperature and humidity are displayed but not used, because BASS-II did not vary them. Low-flow values below the anemometer floor are estimated (see docs). Ember Guard estimates risk; it does not detect fires. Cabin readings are simulated unless live mode is on.
