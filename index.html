Data. 122 ISS tests from NASA's Burning and Suppression of Solids–II (BASS-II) experiment, Microgravity Science Glovebox, 2014: Olson et al., BASS-II Summary Report, NASA/TM-20210011385 (2021), Appendix A. ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20210011385

Model. Logistic regression on O₂, airflow and material family (). Validated on whole tests never seen during training.

Live mode. Real ISS cabin readings from NASA's public ISS Live telemetry stream (US Lab: ppO₂ USLAB000053, ppCO₂ USLAB000055, cabin pressure USLAB000058, temperature USLAB000059), served through Lightstreamer. O₂ % = ppO₂ ÷ cabin pressure.

Limits. Valid for O₂ 13.5–22 %, airflow 0.4–47 cm/s, about 1 atm, and these materials only. CO₂, temperature and humidity are shown but not used, because BASS-II did not vary them. Cabin readings are simulated unless live mode is on. AI (Claude) was used as a programming assistant.
